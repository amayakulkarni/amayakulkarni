### 🌌 About Me

Hi, I'm **Amaya** (she/her). I am an independent systems architect, backend developer, and maker based in Pune, India. 

I care deeply about building reliable, clean, and self reliant resiliant systems avoiding unnecessary SaaS bloat in favor of intentional, modular engineering. Whether I'm configuring kernel-level packet inspection on embedded Linux routers, writing cryptographic webhook bridges, or tuning micro tolerances on an FDM 3D printer, I love turning abstract logic into tangible, reliable realities.

* 🔭 **Founder & Software Developer** at [Veronami](https://veronami.com) (Digital Services & Physical Fabrication Studio).
* ⚙️ **Core Focus:** Node.js backend infrastructure, REST API design, cryptographic data pipelines, and self-hosted Linux deployments.
* 🛰️ **Systems & Networking:** OpenWrt v23 routing, 802.1Q VLAN topologies, Asterisk VoIP proxies, and WireGuard mesh networks.
* 🛠️ **Physical & Spatial:** Parametric CAD modeling, binary mesh parsing (STL/3MF), and functional FDM additive manufacturing.
* 🎮 **Downtime:** Cruising Night City in *Cyberpunk 2077*, digital illustration, and tinkering with smart-home tech.
